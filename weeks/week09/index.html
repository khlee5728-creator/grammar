Week 9

Conditionals & Wish

가정법과 I wish 구문

Units 36 – 39

Learning Objectives

이번 주 학습 목표

  • Distinguish between first, second, and third conditionals 1차, 2차, 3차 조건문을 구별할 수 있다
  • Use the correct tense in if-clauses and result clauses if절과 결과절에 올바른 시제를 사용할 수 있다
  • Form sentences with "I wish" for present and past situations "I wish" 구문으로 현재와 과거 상황을 표현할 수 있다
  • Avoid common Korean-speaker errors with conditional tenses 조건문 시제에서 한국어 화자의 흔한 실수를 피할 수 있다

if I do ... and if I did ... Unit 36

1차 조건문 — 실현 가능한 미래 상황

📖 GIU Unit 36A, p.72-73

A if + present, will + future (first conditional)

Real / Possible Future

Use the first conditional when you think something is a real possibility.

📖 "For Jess, it is possible that they will take the bus, so she says: If we take the bus, it will be ..." (Unit 36A)

If + present simple, ... will + base form
"If we take the bus, it will be cheaper."
The speaker sees this as a real possibility — they might actually take the bus. 화자는 이것을 실제 가능성으로 봅니다 — 실제로 버스를 탈 수도 있습니다.
"If you study hard, you will pass the exam."

if I knew ... Units 36B–37A

2차 조건문 — 비현실적 / 상상의 현재 상황

📖 GIU Units 36B–37A, pp.72-75

B if + past, would + conditional (second conditional)

Unreal / Imaginary Present

Use the second conditional for situations that are not real now or are unlikely.

If + past simple, ... would + base form
"If we took the bus, it would be cheaper." 상상
"I'd call him if I knew his number." 번호를 모름
"If you were in my position, what would you do?"
Use "were" (not "was") for all subjects in formal English.

I wish I knew ... Unit 37

I wish + 과거형 — 현재 상황에 대한 소망

📖 GIU Unit 37B, pp.74-75

B I wish I knew / I wish I were

Wish about the Present

Use I wish + past simple to say you want something to be different now.

I wish + past simple
"I wish I knew Paul's phone number." 번호를 모름
"Do you ever wish you could fly?" 불가능한 소망
"I wish there weren't so many people." 달랐으면 좋겠음

Conditional Rules: were or was after if/wish Unit 37C

if·wish 뒤에는 were / was 둘 다 가능 (were가 더 격식체)

📖 GIU Unit 37C, p.75

C were or was after if/wish (were = more formal)

After if and wish, you can use were instead of was. You can also use was.

📖 "After if and wish, you can use were instead of was. … You can also use was." (Unit 37C)

"If I were you, I wouldn't say it." or "If I was you, …" (were = 더 격식체)
"I wish Anna were here." or "I wish Anna was here."

Conditional Rules: no would in if-part · could/might Units 36C · 37D

if-절에 would 금지 + 결과절에는 could·might 가능

📖 GIU Units 36C / 37D, pp.72-75

D Do NOT use would in the if-part (Unit 37D / 36C)

Use would only in the main clause, not in the if-clause.

📖 "We do not normally use would in the if part of the sentence." (Unit 36C)

"I'd be scared if somebody would point a gun at me." → "I'd be scared if somebody pointed a gun at me."

C could and might are also possible in the result clause (Unit 36C)

"If I won a lot of money, I might buy a house." (maybe / 가능성)
"If it stopped raining, we could go out." (would be able to)

could (= would be able to / was able to) Unit 37E

could의 두 의미 — 조건적 능력 vs 과거 능력

📖 GIU Unit 37E, p.75

E could = "would be able to" / "was able to"

Two Meanings of "could"

In conditional/wish contexts, could often means "would be able to". In past contexts, it means "was/were able to".

📖 "Could sometimes means 'would be able to' and sometimes 'was/were able to'." (Unit 37E)

could = would be able to (조건적) | was/were able to (과거)
"She could get a better job if she could speak English better." (= would be able to get / would be able to speak)
"I wish I could help you." (= I wish I were able to help)
"I could have gone with them, but I was too busy." (= I was able to go but didn't)

if I had known ... Unit 38A–B

3차 조건문 — 비현실적 과거 상황

📖 GIU Unit 38A–B, pp.76-77

A if I had known (third conditional)

Unreal Past

Use the third conditional to talk about past situations that did not happen.

📖 "We use if + had ('d) ... to talk about the past (if I'd known / if you'd done, etc.)." (Unit 38A)

If + past perfect, ... would have + past participle
"If I'd known you were in the hospital, I would have gone to visit you."
I didn't know, so I didn't visit. I'm imagining a different past. 몰랐기 때문에 방문하지 못했습니다. 다른 과거를 상상하고 있습니다.

B would have done (third conditional, continued)

"If I'd seen you, I would have said hello."
I didn't see you when you passed me on the street. 길에서 지나칠 때 당신을 보지 못했습니다.

I wish I had known ... Unit 38C

I wish + 과거완료 — 과거에 대한 후회

📖 GIU Unit 38C, pp.76-77

C I wish I had known (regret about the past)

Regret about the Past

Use I wish + past perfect to express regret about something that already happened.

📖 "I wish something had happened = I am sorry that it didn't happen." (Unit 38C)

I wish + past perfect (had + past participle)
"I wish I'd known that Matt was sick. I would have gone to see him."
I didn't know Matt was sick, so I didn't visit him. Now I regret it. Matt가 아픈 줄 몰랐기 때문에 만나러 가지 않았습니다. 지금 후회합니다.
"I feel sick. I wish I hadn't eaten so much."
I ate too much — now I regret it. 너무 많이 먹었습니다 — 지금 후회합니다.

would have / could have / might have done Unit 38D

결과절의 모달 변형 — 확실성·가능성·잠재성 차이

📖 GIU Unit 38D, p.77

D Compare would have, could have, might have done

Three Modal Variations in 3rd Conditional

All three are used in 3rd conditional result clauses, but with different shades of meaning.

📖 "Compare would have, could have and might have." (Unit 38D)

If + past perfect, ... would / could / might + have + p.p.
"If the weather hadn't been so bad, we would have gone out." 확실 — 갔을 것이다
"If the weather hadn't been so bad, we could have gone out." 가능 — 갈 수 있었을 텐데
"If the weather hadn't been so bad, we might have gone out." 잠재 — 갔을지도 모른다

wish: wish somebody luck ... Unit 39A

wish + 사람 + 명사 — 행운·성공을 빎 (다른 의미의 wish)

📖 GIU Unit 39A, p.78

A wish somebody luck / all the best / success

wish + 사람 + 명사 — 축복 표현 (regret과는 완전히 다른 의미).

📖 "We say 'wish somebody luck / all the best / success, etc.'" (Unit 39A)

"I wish you all the best in the future." 앞날에 행운을 빕니다
"I saw Mark before the exam, and he wished me luck."
주의: wish vs hope (미래의 일은 hope)
"I wish you feel better soon." → "I hope you feel better soon."

I wish ... — regret about present & past Unit 39B

후회 표현 종합 — 현재(과거형) · 과거(과거완료)

📖 GIU Unit 39B, p.78

B wish to express regret — past for present, past perfect for past

When we use wish to regret a situation, we use past for present meaning and past perfect for past meaning. (Combines Units 37B & 38C.)

📖 "We also use wish to say that we regret something, that something is not as we would like it." (Unit 39B)

"I wish I had an umbrella." (= I don't have one — 현재)
"I wish I were going too." (= I'm not going — 현재)
"It was a stupid thing to say. I wish I hadn't said it." (= I did say it — 과거)

Compare: "I'm glad I live here." (긍정) ↔ "I wish I lived somewhere else." (후회)

I wish I could ... / I wish I could have ... Unit 39C

현재 못해서 아쉬움 / 과거에 못해서 후회

📖 GIU Unit 39C, p.78

C I wish I could (do) / I wish I could have (done)

Wishing About Ability — Present vs Past

I wish I could (do) = I regret that I cannot do it (now). I wish I could have (done) = I regret that I could not do it (in the past).

📖 "I wish I could (do something) = I regret that I cannot do it." (Unit 39C)

I wish I + could + base form  |  I wish I + could have + p.p.
"It's a pity I can't go. I wish I could go." 현재 못 가서 아쉬움
"I'm sorry you're not well. I wish I could remember his name, but I can't." 현재 기억 못해서 아쉬움
"I hear the party was great. I wish I could have gone." 과거에 못 가서 후회

wish: I wish ... would Unit 39D–E

I wish + would — 짜증/요청 표현

📖 GIU Unit 39D–E, pp.78-79

D I wish ... would

Annoyance or Request for Change

Use I wish + would when you are annoyed or want something to change.

I wish + subject + would + base form
"I wish it would stop raining." 짜증
"I wish somebody would answer it." 전화가 계속 울림

E I wish ... would (annoyance)

"I wish you wouldn't keep interrupting me."
Note: "I wish I would..." is NOT used. Say "I wish I could..." instead.

Comparing Conditionals Units 36–38

조건문 비교 — 같은 상황, 세 가지 조건문

📖 GIU Units 36–38 (Summary)

Three Conditional Types Compared (Units 36–38 Summary)

1st Conditional

Real possibility

If + present, will + base

"If we take the bus, it will be cheaper."
We might really take the bus.

2nd Conditional

Imaginary now

If + past, would + base

"If we took the bus, it would be cheaper."
We're not going to take it.

3rd Conditional

Imaginary past

If + past perfect, would have + pp

"If we had taken the bus, it would have been cheaper."
We didn't take it — too late now.

Common Mistakes for Korean Speakers

한국어 화자가 자주 하는 실수

1/2

L1 Interference Warning

Korean has no conditional tense changes. Students often use present tense where English requires past tense.

"If I am rich, I would buy a house."
"If I were rich, I would buy a house." if절에 과거형
Mixing Conditionals

Don't use "will" in the if-clause of first conditionals.

"If I will have time, I go." → "If I have time, I will go."

Common Mistakes for Korean Speakers

한국어 화자가 자주 하는 실수

2/2

Wish + Present Tense

Korean speakers may say "I wish I know" instead of "I wish I knew."

"I wish I know the answer." → "I wish I knew the answer."
Wish about the present uses past tense. 현재에 대한 소망은 과거형을 사용합니다.
