Learning Objectives

이번 주 학습 목표

  • Form passive sentences in all major tenses 모든 주요 시제에서 수동태 문장을 만들 수 있다
  • Use reporting structures: "It is said that..." / "He is said to..." 보고 구문을 사용할 수 있다: "It is said that..." / "He is said to..."
  • Use the causative: "have/get something done" 사역 구문을 사용할 수 있다: "have/get something done"
  • Choose correctly between active and passive voice 능동태와 수동태를 올바르게 선택할 수 있다

Passive 1 — Active vs Passive Unit 40A-B

능동 vs 수동 — 행위자 vs 대상

📖 GIU Unit 40A-B, p.80

A Active focuses on WHO does it · Passive focuses on WHAT happens

Active: "My grandfather built this house in 1981." → Passive: "This house was built in 1981."
Active: "It's a big company. It employs two hundred people." → Passive: "Two hundred people are employed by the company."

B Use passive when WHO/WHAT does the action is unknown or unimportant

If we want to say WHO did it, we use by. 행위자가 중요할 때만 by 사용.

"A lot of money was stolen in the robbery." (행위자 unknown)
"This house was built by my grandfather." (by + 행위자 — 중요할 때)

Passive 1 — be + past participle Unit 40C

수동태 기본 형태 — 단순 현재 / 단순 과거

📖 GIU Unit 40C, p.80

C Passive = be (am/is/are/was/were) + past participle (done, cleaned, built, seen, ...)

Subject + be (correct tense) + past participle (+ by agent)
Simple Present: "Many accidents are caused by careless driving." / "This room is cleaned every day."
Simple Past: "We were woken up by a loud noise during the night." / "This house was built in 1981."
Irregular past participles (Appendix 1): build → built, steal → stolen, take → taken, etc.

Passive 2 — Modal · Perfect forms Unit 41A-C

조동사·완료 수동태

📖 GIU Unit 41A-C, p.82

A After will / can / must / going to / want to → (to) be + done

"The situation is serious. Something must be done."
"A new supermarket is going to be built next year."

B After should / might / would have / seem to have → (to) have been + done

"It might have been sent to the wrong address." 잘못된 주소로 보내졌을 수 있다
"There were some problems at first, but they seem to have been solved."

C Present Perfect / Past Perfect Passive — have/has been + done / had been + done

"Have you ever been bitten by a dog?" / "The meeting has been canceled."
"The vegetables didn't taste good. They had been cooked too long." (past perfect passive)

Passive 2 — Continuous forms Unit 41D

진행형 수동태

📖 GIU Unit 41D, p.82

D Present Continuous / Past Continuous Passive — am/is/are being + done / was/were being + done

Active: "Somebody is cleaning the room at the moment." → Passive: "The room is being cleaned at the moment."
"There's somebody walking behind us. I think we are being followed."
"There was somebody walking behind us. I think we were being followed." (past continuous passive)
"A new bridge is being built across the river. It will be finished next year."

Passive 3 — Two-object verbs & passive gerund Unit 42A-B

두 개의 목적어 동사 + being done (수동 동명사)

📖 GIU Unit 42A-B, p.84

A I was offered ... / We were given ... (two-object verbs: ask · offer · pay · show · tell · give)

Active: "My grandfather gave me this watch." → Passive: "I was given this watch." or "This watch was given to me."
"I've been offered the job, but I don't think I'll accept it."

B I don't like being ... (passive of doing/seeing = being done / being seen)

Active: "I don't like people telling me what to do." → Passive: "I don't like being told what to do."
"I remember being taken to the zoo when I was a child."

Passive 3 — be born & get + past participle Unit 42C-D

be born (출생) + get + p.p. (비격식 수동태)

📖 GIU Unit 42C-D, p.84

C I was born ... (always past for birth)

"I was born in Chicago." / "Where were you born?" (NOT "Where are you born?")
"How many babies are born every day?" (현재 — 출생 일반 사실)

D get + past participle (informal — instead of be)

"There was a fight, but nobody got hurt." (= nobody was hurt)
"I'm surprised Liz didn't get offered the job." / get married, get divorced, get dressed, get lost

⚠️ Use get only when things happen or change. Don't use get for states/situations.

📖 "We use get only when things happen or change." (Unit 42D)

"Jessica gets liked by everybody." → "Jessica is liked by everybody." (상태 — 'happening' 아님)
"Very little got known about him." → "Very little was known about him." (상태 묘사)

It is said that ... He is said to ... Unit 43A

보고 구문 — 두 가지 수동 보고 패턴

📖 GIU Unit 43A, p.86

A It is said that ... / He is said to ...

It + is said/believed/reported + that + clause
Subject + is said/believed + to + base form

📖 Other reporting verbs: alleged · believed · considered · expected · known · reported · thought · understood. (Unit 43A)

"It is said that he is 108 years old." or "He is said to be 108 years old." (same meaning)
"It is expected that the strike will end soon." or "The strike is expected to end soon."
"The thieves are thought to have gotten in through a window." (have + p.p. = 과거 사건; AmE gotten / BrE got)

supposed to ... — three meanings Unit 43B

supposed to의 세 가지 의미

📖 GIU Unit 43B, p.86

B supposed to ... — three meanings

(1) = said to (사람들이 그렇게 말함 — Part A 확장)

(2) = intended / arranged / expected (실제 상황과 다른 경우 많음)

(3) not supposed to = not allowed / advised (허용되지 않음 / 권장되지 않음)

(1) "I want to see that movie. It's supposed to be good." (= people say it's good)
(1) "Fireworks are supposed to have been invented in China." (have been + p.p. = 과거 사건)
(2) "What are you doing at work? You're supposed to be on vacation." (= 휴가 중이어야 하는데)
(3) "You're not supposed to park your car here. It's private parking only."

have something done / get something done Unit 44

사역 구문 — 누군가에게 시키다

📖 GIU Unit 44A-D, p.88–89

A have something done = arrange for somebody else to do it

Use this when you arrange for someone else to do something for you. (직접 X — 시켜서 함)

"Megan had the roof repaired." (= she arranged for somebody else to repair it) cf. "Megan repaired the roof" = she did it herself

B Word order: have + object + past participle

"Where did you have your hair cut?" / "How often do you have your car serviced?" (NOT "have serviced your car")

C get something done (informal — instead of have)

"When are you going to get the roof repaired?" / "I think you should get your hair cut."

D have something done — different meaning (something happens to you)

"Nick had his nose broken in a fight." ⚠️ 사역이 아닌 — 코가 부러지는 일이 일어남

Common Mistakes for Korean Speakers

한국어 화자의 흔한 실수

L1 Interference Warning

Korean uses passive markers (-되다/-지다) differently. Students often overuse "by" or forget "be".

"The cake was eaten by me." → "The cake was eaten."
Only use "by..." when the agent is important. 행위자가 중요할 때만 "by..."를 사용.
Missing "be" in Passive
"The window broken yesterday." → "The window was broken yesterday."
Passive always needs a form of "be." 수동태에는 반드시 "be"가 필요합니다.

When to Use the Passive

수동태를 사용하는 경우

1. Agent is unknown

"My bike was stolen."

행위자를 모를 때

2. Agent is obvious

"He was arrested."

행위자가 명백할 때 (경찰)

3. Agent is unimportant

"The building was completed in 2020."

행위자가 중요하지 않을 때

4. Formal / academic writing

"The data were analyzed using SPSS."

격식체/학술적 글쓰기

Week 10 Summary

10주차 요약

  • Passive formula: Subject + be + past participle (+ by agent) 수동태 공식: 주어 + be + 과거분사 (+ by 행위자)
  • Passive works across all tenses — change the form of "be" 모든 시제에서 수동태 가능 — "be"의 형태를 바꿈
  • Reporting: "It is said that..." / "He is said to..." 보고 구문: "It is said that..." / "He is said to..."
  • Causative: "have/get + object + past participle" 사역 구문: "have/get + 목적어 + 과거분사"
  • Only include "by..." when the agent is important 행위자가 중요할 때만 "by..."를 포함
